This is the app's own workspace frame. The panel should cover exactly this box - not the rail, not the tab bar, not the window edges - because it measures the frame instead of recomputing the shell's arithmetic.

The gauge icon in the rail is the panel. Click it, then resize the window or collapse the rail and watch the panel follow the frame.

Settings are saved to cookies named fbop_*, which is what the Go guard reads.
